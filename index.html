🌍 Underrepresented Regions
Africa
Holds 25% of global disease burden but <3% of clinical trials. Sub-Saharan Africa critically underrepresented.
MENA Region
Tunisia, Morocco, Algeria: combined ~400 trials vs thousands in France or Germany for similar diseases.
Southeast Asia
High burden of infectious disease & NCD with limited Phase 3 trial infrastructure outside major metros.
⚡ Key Epidemiology Facts
Cancer
19.3M new cases/year (WHO 2022). ~160,000 active trials globally. Most studied: breast, lung, colorectal.
Cardiovascular Disease
Leading cause of death: 17.9M deaths/year. ~80,000 trials. Strong in Europe & North America.
Infectious Disease
Malaria: 249M cases (2022). HIV: 39M living with virus. TB: 10.6M new cases. Africa hosts most burden but few trials.
Neurological
Alzheimer affects 55M globally. Parkinson: 10M. MS: 2.8M. Major unmet need in low-income countries.
